1. Lead Intake and New Client Onboarding
This module helps your firm capture and manage potential clients from multiple sources and convert them into active matters in a structured way.
The system allows you to capture leads from website forms, manual entry by staff, phone inquiries, advertisements, and referrals. All leads are organized into an intake pipeline where they can be tracked through stages such as New Lead, Contacted, Consultation Scheduled, Qualified, Hired, and Closed Lost.
You can create custom intake forms for different practice areas to collect relevant information based on the type of case. The system also supports automated follow-ups through email and SMS to ensure timely communication with potential clients.
Clients can schedule consultations and appointments directly, with calendar synchronization to avoid conflicts. Before opening a matter, the system allows you to perform a conflict check to ensure compliance.
Each lead record includes notes, tags, source tracking, and assigned staff members for better management. The platform also supports e-signatures for engagement letters and onboarding forms.
Once a lead is confirmed, it can be easily converted into a client and a matter. The system also provides reports on marketing sources and conversion performance to help you understand which channels are most effective.

2. Matter and Case Management
This module provides a centralized system to manage all client matters and case-related activities.
You can create and manage clients and matters, and define practice areas and matter types based on your firm’s requirements. Each matter includes status and stage tracking so you can monitor progress easily.
A dedicated matter dashboard gives a complete overview of all related information, including documents, notes, timelines, tasks, and billing details. The system also allows you to manage contacts such as clients, opposing counsel, witnesses, experts, and court information in one place.
All case activities are recorded in a chronological timeline, ensuring a clear history of actions taken. You can also define custom fields for different matter types to capture specific information.
Internal notes can be added for team collaboration, and important updates can be pinned for quick visibility. Documents and files are organized within each matter for easy access.
The system includes role-based access control, allowing different levels of access for lawyers, paralegals, administrative staff, and billing teams.

3. Task, Calendar, and Deadline Management
This module helps your team stay organized and ensures that all tasks and deadlines are properly managed.
You can create tasks and assign them to team members, with clear tracking of progress and status. The system supports reusable task templates for different case types, which helps standardize workflows and save time.
Deadlines can be set for tasks, with automatic reminders to ensure nothing is missed. A centralized calendar allows you to manage hearings, meetings, filing dates, and follow-ups.
The calendar can be viewed at both individual and team levels, providing full visibility of schedules. Appointments and events can be linked directly to specific matters.
A notification system alerts users about upcoming deadlines and overdue tasks, helping the team stay on track.

4. Document Management and Document Automation
This module provides a secure and efficient way to manage all legal documents.
Documents are stored and organized within each matter, with support for folder structures and tagging for easy navigation. The system includes document templates for common legal documents such as engagement letters, notices, motions, agreements, invoices, and standard letters.
Templates can be automatically filled using client and matter data, reducing manual work and improving accuracy. The system also maintains a history of document versions, allowing you to track changes over time.
You can request documents directly from clients through the platform, and manage court-form generation using guided workflows. E-signature functionality is available for signing documents digitally.
Documents can be downloaded, shared, or sent for review securely.

5. Time Tracking, Billing, and Payments
This module helps manage time tracking, billing processes, and payment collection efficiently.
Users can record both billable and non-billable time entries and link them to specific matters. Expenses related to matters can also be tracked and recorded.
The system supports different billing models, including flat fees, hourly rates, and mixed billing structures. Invoices can be generated based on recorded time and expenses.
It also supports applying trust balances to invoices where applicable. Online payment links can be shared with clients for easy payment processing.
The system tracks all payments and generates receipts. It also provides visibility into outstanding balances and unpaid invoices.
Billing reports and collection summaries are available to help monitor financial performance.

6. Trust Accounting and Legal Finance
This module ensures proper handling of client funds and compliance with legal accounting requirements.
The system maintains separate ledgers for trust accounts and operating accounts. It tracks trust balances for each matter, including retainer deposits and usage.
All trust-related transactions, such as deposits, payments, and transfers, are recorded and managed within the system. Funds can be transferred from trust accounts to operating accounts after billing.
The system also supports handling refunds and disbursements. Reconciliation tools are available to ensure accuracy between system records and bank accounts.
Compliance-ready reports can be generated for audits and regulatory requirements. A financial dashboard provides an overview of receivables, expenses, and account balances.
The platform is also designed to integrate with external accounting systems through export or synchronization features.

7. Reporting and Dashboards
This module provides detailed reports and dashboards to help your firm make informed decisions.
You can generate reports on lead sources to understand where your clients are coming from, as well as intake conversion reports to track how leads progress through the pipeline.
Matter status reports provide insights into ongoing and completed cases. The system also offers reports on lawyer productivity and workload.
Time tracking and billing reports help measure realization and efficiency. Revenue and collection reports provide a clear picture of financial performance.
Trust balance and reconciliation reports ensure transparency in financial management. In addition, team performance dashboards give an overview of overall productivity and operational efficiency.

8. Communication

This module provides a centralized and secure way to manage all communication between the firm, clients, and external parties.
The system maintains a complete communication history for each matter, including emails, messages, call notes, and internal discussions. All interactions are automatically linked to the relevant client or matter, ensuring nothing is lost and everything is easy to track.
You can send and receive emails directly from the platform, with support for syncing with external providers such as Gmail or Outlook. SMS and in-app messaging are also supported, allowing timely communication with clients without relying on external tools.
Each matter includes a dedicated communication thread where team members can view past conversations, add notes, and collaborate internally. Internal notes remain private to the firm, while client-facing messages can be securely shared through the platform.
The system also supports client notifications for important updates such as upcoming appointments, document requests, invoice reminders, and case status changes. Automated communication workflows can be configured to ensure consistent follow-ups and reduce manual effort.
Clients can access a secure portal to view messages, share documents, and communicate with the firm in a structured and professional manner. This improves transparency, reduces back-and-forth emails, and enhances the overall client experience.
All communication is logged and searchable, providing a clear audit trail of interactions for reference, compliance, and dispute resolution if needed.
